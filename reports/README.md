# Reports

This directory contains reports and visualization artifacts generated by the project.

## Subdirectories

- `figures/`: Contains 17 publication-quality visualization charts (300 DPI) exported during EDA:
  - `group1_target_distribution.png`: Target class distribution (Bar chart & Donut chart).
  - `group2_num_city_development_index.png`: CDI distribution and boxplot by target.
  - `group2_num_training_hours.png`: Training hours distribution and boxplot by target.
  - `group3_nominal_company_type.png`: Distribution and target rate for company type.
  - `group3_nominal_enrolled_university.png`: Distribution and target rate for university enrollment.
  - `group3_nominal_gender.png`: Distribution and target rate for gender.
  - `group3_nominal_major_discipline.png`: Distribution and target rate for major discipline.
  - `group3_nominal_relevent_experience.png`: Distribution and target rate for relevant experience.
  - `group4_ordinal_company_size.png`: Ordinal distribution and target rate for company size.
  - `group4_ordinal_education_level.png`: Ordinal distribution and target rate for education level.
  - `group4_ordinal_experience.png`: Line chart depicting monotonic decrease in target rate across experience years.
  - `group4_ordinal_last_new_job.png`: Ordinal distribution and target rate for last new job.
  - `group5_high_cardinality_city_top15.png`: Top 15 cities horizontal bar charts.
  - `group6_missing_values_bar.png`: Missing percentage and sample count per column.
  - `group6_missingness_vs_target.png`: Target rate comparison between observed vs missing values (MNAR test).
  - `group6_correlation_matrix.png`: 7x7 Spearman rank correlation heatmap.
  - `group7_pca_scree_plot.png`: PCA scree plot and cumulative explained variance curve (90% and 95% thresholds).
